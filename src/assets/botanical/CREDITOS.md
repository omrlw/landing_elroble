```

Keep the reference's olive and sage colors, matte painted naturalism, tiny subtle grain, fine veins and smooth leaf margins. The difference is silhouette and direction only. Use a natural variety of greens closely matching the reference with a quiet soft highlight along one or two leaves. Strictly olive-green monochrome, including the stem. No brown wood, trunk, flowers, fruit, acorns, autumn colors, yellow or orange accents, strokes or ink outlines. Show the complete branch with enough clean space around every edge, landscape aspect ratio around 1.7:1. Transparent alpha inside and outside the silhouette, no backdrop, no glow, no drop shadow, no text.
```

## Patrón de fondo

[olive-pattern.webp](olive-pattern.webp)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a transparent landscape tile for a quiet website wallpaper of individually spaced botanical sprigs, all in the exact same aesthetic as the attached reference. Use only olive green and sage, delicate pale green veins, subtle natural botanical grain, and smooth pointed leaves like the reference. Make five SMALL independent sprigs with 2–4 leaves each, alternating gentle diagonals and scales in two loose staggered rows: three across the upper half and two offset in the lower half. Each element is isolated with abundant transparent negative space between all elements and generous empty outer margins so the tile repeats without cut stems or recognizable seams. Avoid a regular icon grid; form a soft airy rhythm. Keep the tiny stems olive. No flowers, acorns, fruits, tree, bark, brown, ochre, yellow, colored backdrop, paper texture, shadows, border, text, watermark, or overlapping motifs. True transparent alpha so the tile can sit on cream and forest backgrounds.
```
